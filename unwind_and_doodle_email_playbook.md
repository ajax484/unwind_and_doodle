Purpose of this document
This playbook contains the complete retention strategy and approved email copy developed for Unwind and Doodle. It is designed as a working reference. Copy the relevant email, replace any placeholders, add the correct link and send it only to the audience described.
The strategy is built around one central finding: customers like the products, but there was no structured system giving them a reason to return. The email system must therefore help customers use what they bought, introduce the next logical product and announce every genuinely new colouring edition without overwhelming the list.
Key findings from the Bumpa order history
476 clean paid orders were analysed after removing provisional internal and incomplete records.
The file contained 441 unique customers and 435 usable email addresses.
98.7 percent of clean paid orders included an email address.
The surface repeat-customer rate was 6.35 percent.
Only eight customers repeated after 30 days or more. The more meaningful 30-day repeat rate was approximately 1.8 percent.
Several quick repeat orders appeared to be split orders, add-ons or bulk purchases rather than genuine long-term retention.
The retention opportunity is therefore substantial, especially because every new colouring-book batch contains completely new pages.

Sending rules
Send no more than two promotional emails in one week.
Transactional order and delivery messages do not count as promotional emails.
Remove customers from a sales sequence immediately after they purchase the promoted product.
Do not send a win-back email during an active launch campaign.
Do not promote a product to somebody who has just bought it.
Imported Bumpa customers must retain their real consent status. Do not mark every imported customer as subscribed automatically.
Every promotional email must include an unsubscribe link.
Low-stock messages must only be sent when stock is genuinely low.
Do not train inactive customers to wait for automatic discounts.
Use one main button or action in each email.

Two sales emails
One nurture email
One founder, customer or behind-the-scenes email
During a product launch, three or four sales emails may be sent close together. Reduce communication afterwards. Do not run several product sequences at the same time.
Developer implementation checklist
1. Complete multi-email automation sequences with separate delays.
2. Add product-specific purchase triggers and customer segments.
3. Add combined rules such as purchased Product A, has not purchased Product B and last order was more than 30 days ago.
4. Add exit conditions so customers stop receiving a sales sequence after purchase.
5. Prevent customers from entering the same automation repeatedly by mistake.
6. Trigger post-purchase journeys after delivery where possible, not immediately after payment.
7. Import Bumpa customers with their product history, order dates and consent status.
8. Match historical Bumpa product names to the corresponding products on the new website.
9. Add test-email functionality.
10. Add delivery, open, click, unsubscribe, bounce and attributed-sales reporting.
11. Confirm that unsubscribe and suppression rules work across campaigns.
12. Authenticate the sending domain and test deliverability before sending to the full list.
Testing checklist before activation
1. Create a test customer using an email address controlled by the business.
2. Subscribe through the actual website form.
3. Confirm the welcome email arrives with the correct name, links and sender information.
4. Abandon a checkout and verify both reminders and exit behaviour after payment.
5. Complete a test purchase for each main product category.
6. Confirm the correct product-specific segment and recommendation are assigned.
7. Confirm a purchase removes the test customer from active sales and win-back sequences.
8. Test the unsubscribe link and verify suppression from future promotional emails.
9. Check every email on both mobile and desktop before activation.
10. Review performance weekly during the first month and fix any incorrect triggers immediately.
